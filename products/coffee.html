Why this product

Grown at 25°N — the northern edge of the world's coffee belt, sharing its latitude with celebrated origins like Mexico — Yunman Estate sits at 1,500m in the misty, volcanic-soil mountains of Lushui, Nujiang. The estate is certified organic (in conversion, Cert. No. 375OP2501270) and focuses on small-batch, shade-grown cultivation rather than volume.

Our Origin

A gifted land on the golden latitude: Yunman Estate covers roughly 2,760 mu (about 184 hectares) at an average elevation above 1,400m in Shangjiang, Lushui — wrapped in cloud year-round, with gentle sun and cool air that give the beans their elegant, layered flavor.
